Nagrik

Report civic problems to the right local office.

Nagrik helps residents report potholes, broken streetlights, garbage, water leaks and drainage problems. It finds the responsible municipal office, writes the complaint, files it, and follows it up.

Nagrik is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government body. Complaints are filed on official portals such as the Wanaparthy Municipality grievance portal and the CDMA Telangana grievance portal (cdma.telangana.gov.in).
