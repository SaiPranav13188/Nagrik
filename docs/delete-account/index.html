Nagrik app

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nagrik and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your data is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email saipranav1388@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Nagrik account. We will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, name, mobile number and email
  • All your complaints, their photos, status history and escalation letters
  • Your notifications and "Me too" supports
  • Records of the AI steps that processed your complaints

What we cannot delete

Complaints already filed with a government office stay in that office's own system, and escalation emails already sent stay with their recipients. Contact the office directly to have those removed.
